# Instituto Geográfico Nacional, Spain (`ign`)

IGN's [tide-gauge network](https://www.ign.es/web/gds-red-mareografos) publishes observations and reviewed records through an [open HTTPS directory](https://datos-geodesia.ign.es/Red_de_Mareografos/). The network maintains Spain's height references. The store includes its twelve gauges, including the retired FUER1 and MURC1, and keeps historical chart records and unlinked raw references as separate series of those gauges.

- **License:** the [server README](https://datos-geodesia.ign.es/Red_de_Mareografos/Leeme.txt) requires attribution to Área de Geodesia, IGN and states no other condition on the series. The source permits redistribution with that credit and is tagged non-commercial pending IGN's confirmation that its broader CC BY 4.0 policy covers these observations. The investigation and supporting license documents are in [issue #181](https://github.com/openwatersio/stillwell/issues/181). The adapter does not assert that the tide series are CC BY 4.0.
- **Contact for errors:** ign@transportes.gob.es, published in IGN's website footer; the data README also gives buzon-geodesia@fomento.es.
- **Station metadata:** positions, numeric file codes, reference marks, orthometric heights and sensor codes come from the [station sheets](https://datos-geodesia.ign.es/Red_de_Mareografos/fichas/). The root listing checks the supported gauge inventory; a changed inventory needs its sheets checked before the catalog refresh proceeds. Ten gauges are operational according to their sheets; ACOR1 and ALBO1 can nevertheless have empty current-year directories. FUER1 stopped in 2018 and MURC1 in 2005.
- **Series:** `ign/ALAC1`, for example, is the gauge's reviewed JM series. For a gauge with a published BM height, raw digital readings are converted to JM and join it. ALAC1, ALAC2, FUER2 and MURC1 have no BM height in their sheets: their digital distances stay in separate `-BM` series, increasing upward from BM, with no assumed BM–JM offset. Historical chart records have arbitrary instrument zeros and stay in separate sensor series such as `ign/ALAC1-0301`, in `ZERO`; they do not inherit the gauge's JM or national-datum offsets. Every series of a gauge names its other series in `same_as`.
- **Levels:** millimeters become meters. Digital raw files give distances downward from BM, so their sign is reversed; converting one to JM adds BM's orthometric height minus JM's. Historical chart readings keep their published sign. Reviewed files explicitly name JM or BM in their headers; BM files use the surveyed BM–JM difference. A header naming another gauge, reference or unit fails the fetch. Battery voltage (`2000`) and atmospheric pressure (`1501`) are excluded by sensor selection and unit validation.
- **Reviewed coverage:** both `procesados/integros` and `procesados/horarios` are read. The first wins at shared timestamps, with the hourly series supplying years and hours it lacks. In the November 2026 inventory, ALAC2's minute files start in 2024 but its hourly files start in 2000; MURC1 has a reviewed hourly file for 2001. ALBO1, TN021 and TN031 have reviewed BM files, which are converted using their sheets. FUER1's minute files change from JM to BM in 2009. The adapter checks each file's own reference rather than assuming a folder-wide datum.
- **Raw coverage:** daily files are listed by year. Historical requests longer than a week prefer the sensor's annual exports in `observados/ficheros_anuales`; where none exists, daily files are read. Sensor priority follows the station sheet: the newer radar first, then the older radar and digital encoder. One sensor supplies a day's valid readings; another is used only when it supplies none that day. This keeps a spare sensor's readings out of the primary sensor's gaps. Historical chart sensors are read individually, so a change of instrument does not silently reuse an arbitrary zero.
- **Flags:** reviewed values are `good`; IGN says they include interpolation and corrections but does not give a per-value qualifier to distinguish those changes. Raw values remain `preliminary`. `9999`, `-9999` and `---` are missing and dropped. A reviewed value wins over a raw value at the same time; finer raw timestamps are retained. A raw series without a surveyed link to JM cannot be finalized by a reviewed JM file and remains preliminary.
- **Times:** timestamps are read as UTC, without a seasonal shift. Alicante's reviewed record and the store's CMEMS copy in the first week of January 2024 fit best without a clock shift: 152 shared samples, 1.79 cm RMS after removing the mean offset, against 2.16 cm one hour earlier and 2.17 cm one hour later. This sample does not establish every historical chart's clock. The parser refuses impossible dates, timestamps outside the requested file's year or day, conflicting repeated timestamps and decreasing timestamps. The returned interval includes its start and excludes its end.
- **Datums:** each canonical series is relative to its sheet's Join Mark. Its `datums` gives the mark's orthometric height relative to the applicable height origin: `NMMA` on the peninsula, `Tenerife`, `Fuerteventura` or `Alboran` on those islands. IGN's [official height-origin table](https://datos-geodesia.ign.es/Red_de_Mareografos/fichas/Origenes_Altitudes_Nacionales.pdf) explicitly distinguishes these origins. Island heights are never labeled NMMA. Both Alicante Join Marks are NP1, given to the centimeter as 3.41 m in their sheets; that is the precision of the adapter's NMMA offset.
- **Copies:** links to GESLA's IGN records use the identities and positions documented in issue #181. Shared ranking chooses the preferred series; IGN is preferred to GESLA when their commercial-use tags agree. A commercially usable CMEMS series can remain preferred while IGN's commercial-use confirmation is pending.

## Errata

- **Repeated logger rows** (erratum `ign-duplicate-raw-rows`). Identical adjacent raw rows are kept once; a repeated timestamp with conflicting values still fails the fetch.

## Running ingest

Requests start at least 350 ms apart. Inventories and files are read fresh because IGN does not establish that observations or annual exports are immutable. A failed request or malformed listing fails the fetch. A missing optional annual-export directory falls back to daily files; a year absent from the observed-years listing is not requested. A listing that is syntactically complete but omits some files can shorten the response; `keepsStored` preserves values not returned, but cannot fill files the server omitted.

The registry enables hourly reads of live series, daily compaction, station refresh and the monthly preliminary refetch. A preliminary `-BM` or chart series may stay preliminary indefinitely because its reference differs from the reviewed series. Annual exports reduce historical request counts; current-year backfills use daily files and can require hundreds of requests per gauge. No ingest against the public store is needed to test the adapter.

The canary requires both recent Alicante BM observations and reviewed Alicante JM values. Tests use recorded excerpts of the directory, the 2024 JM file and the 1927 chart record; the excerpts retain the upstream text and have no network dependency.

### Backfill

Refresh the station list first, then include the inactive gauges for a complete history:

```bash
node scripts/ingest.ts backfill ign all 1927 2026 --with-inactive --skip-written
node scripts/ingest.ts backfill ign all 2026 2027 --with-inactive
```

The end year is exclusive. The separately referenced chart and BM series are part of `all`; consumers should group them through `same_as` instead of counting them as extra physical gauges.
