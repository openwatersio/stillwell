# GESLA coverage

Generated by `npm run gesla:coverage` from the store's station lists and years files; don't edit by hand. [coverage.csv](coverage.csv) has one row per record. It counts GESLA 4.1's 6656 records in the store (less the records in `DROPPED`, whose values are another gauge's), and for each finds the originator's series of the gauge through `same_as`: any other source's series with levels, except UHSLC's series at a gauge GESLA also holds from another agency, such as BOM or NOAA, whose originator that agency is; those are listed apart as relays (`uhslc_relay`), for every record of the gauge, GESLA's UHSLC records included. Elsewhere UHSLC is the originator. Where a gauge has several originator series, such as a USGS and a CO-OPS series, a year counts if any has it. A year counts on a side when the store has a file of levels for it, so a year of a few hours counts as much as a whole one, and a file with no values doesn't count; a year counts whatever its values' flags. A record is live when hourly ingest polls one of its originator series (`live` and `active`).

GESLA often holds a gauge more than once, from two contributors or in several versions, so the CSV's `gesla_only_years` are one record's years the originator lacks, and `originator_only_years` the originator's years no GESLA record of the gauge has. Station-year totals below count each gauge once.

The usgs and coops history backfills were still running when this was generated (coops 408 of 2930 stations with years, usgs 11959 of 13972 stations with years), so their gaps will shrink. 312 records' series are marked in the years file (`years_marked`): a run was rewriting them, or stopped partway or couldn't read a file, so their years may be short. Regenerate with `npm run gesla:coverage` after a backfill campaign, first taking a finished source out of `BACKFILLING` and updating `REACH` in [the script](../../../scripts/gesla-coverage.ts); it reads the public store, or a local one through `STILLWELL_STORE`.

- **4610** of 6656 records are linked to the originator's series, and **2164** of those to one hourly ingest polls. Another **311** have only UHSLC's copy.
- **2042** linked records have years their originator lacks: **16366** station-years over 1730 gauges. **2046** records have no originator series, **32926** station-years.
- Originators have **3334** station-years GESLA lacks, 1342 of them after GESLA's last year.

## Year gaps by source

Linked gauges, each once, by the source holding the originator's first year, or the first originator series where none has years yet. "Not backfilled" counts gauges whose originator series have no years in the store yet, and the GESLA years they hold. The rest split GESLA's extra years by where they fall against the originator's years in the store: before its first year, in a gap between its first and last, or after its last. Years before the first are a backfill shortfall where the source serves them, and years only GESLA has where it doesn't. "Reach" says which, from the source's README or a spot check through the adapter, on 2026-11-12, of one week of July at one or two gauges, in a year GESLA has; a check speaks for the source's other gauges only as a sample.

Of the 16366 GESLA-only station-years at linked gauges, 10304 (not backfilled, or before the first year) are at gauges one of whose sources serves them, by the checks: a backfill shortfall. 3672 are before the first year at gauges whose every source, by the checks, doesn't reach them, so GESLA is the store's only copy. 1466 are still open: at gauges whose originator series have no years yet at a source no check shows serving them, or at sources without a settled check, such as USGS, whose backfill is still running, and CRMS, whose site's later gauge would cover most. The other 924 fall in gaps or after the originator's last year.

At CRMS, 350 of the GESLA-only years were in a later gauge of the site (`H02`), which GESLA's records didn't link to on 2026-11-12. The rest are years CPRA's export doesn't give for the series it lists: before the site's first listed series (366 years), after its last (106), or at a site whose listed series has no values (140). Some of the years before the first series are the site's marsh well (`W01`), which CPRA gives and the adapter leaves out; the GESLA adapter flags them (erratum `gesla-crms0008-marsh-well` and eight like it). CRMS0176's and CRMS1446's later years are likely another site's gauge, which CPRA lists under the site as `H0X` and the adapter leaves out ([crms README](../crms/README.md)).
