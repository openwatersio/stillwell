# University of Hawaii Sea Level Center (`uhslc`)

Hourly sea level from the University of Hawaii Sea Level Center's [ERDDAP](https://uhslc.soest.hawaii.edu/erddap/): the Joint Archive for Sea Level research-quality data set (RQ, `global_hourly_rqds`) and the fast-delivery data set (FD, `global_hourly_fast`), about 740 series from some 590 gauges worldwide, back to 1846. [UHSLC's notes](https://uhslc.soest.hawaii.edu/datainfo/) describe both.

- **License:** the files say "The data may be used and redistributed for free but is not intended for legal use, since it may contain inaccuracies", with no warranty from the contributor, NOAA or the US government. That allows redistribution but doesn't say whether commercial use is allowed, and UHSLC hasn't yet answered whether it is (see the plan's open questions). So `commercial_use` is false, and every series goes to the restricted lake (`WATER_LEVELS_RESTRICTED_LAKE`), never the public one. UHSLC asks users to cite Caldwell, Merrifield and Thompson (2015), [doi:10.7289/V5V40S7W](https://doi.org/10.7289/V5V40S7W), and for FD data the date through which it was obtained; `terms` carries both. Attribution is "University of Hawaii Sea Level Center (UHSLC)".
- **South Africa:** the South African Navy Hydrographic Office (SANHO) owns and holds copyright in its gauges' data and asks anyone downloading it for permission first; UHSLC's own download page shows that notice before serving any station in South Africa. Stations UHSLC lists in South Africa (ISO 3166-1 numeric 710, 11 stations including Marion Island) aren't listed. Namibia's Lüderitz and Walvis Bay aren't covered by that notice and stay.
- **RQ and FD:** RQ data is a station's record after UHSLC's level 2 assessment (datum stability, benchmark ties, comparison with neighbors), released about once a year. A station's record is split into versions, A to E, wherever UHSLC couldn't tie two segments to one benchmark, so each version has a zero of its own. FD data has passed only the level 1 assessment (units, timing, outliers) and arrives 4 to 6 weeks after it's measured. UHSLC's FD files are already one series per station: its latest RQ version, with FD values appended after the last RQ value, and RQ replaces FD as each year's release comes out. Checked at all 294 FD stations with RQ data: the FD series starts on the same day as the station's latest RQ version, and at Pohnpei its 2020 values are identical to version C's.
- **Stations:** so each FD series is a station, `uhslc/<number>` (`uhslc/001`), that carries its station's latest RQ version, and each RQ version it doesn't carry is a station of its own, `uhslc/<number><version>` (`uhslc/001a`, `uhslc/001b`): the older versions, on other benchmarks, and every version of a station with no FD series, such as `uhslc/230a` (Abidjan). A value is stored once, except where the next paragraph's version change leaves the older version in two places. `same_as` links every series with the same station number. TICON names records the same way, so its FD record `pohnpei-001-fsm-uhslc_fd` is `uhslc/001` and its RQ record `pohnpei-001a-fsm-uhslc_rq` is `uhslc/001a`; its record of a station's latest version (`pohnpei-001c-fsm-uhslc_rq`) is part of `uhslc/001`, whose `version` (kept in the lake's list) says which version that is. Positions are UHSLC's, with longitudes turned from 0–360 to ±180; country codes come from UHSLC's ISO numeric codes. Every station is `coastal`, hourly, and not live.
- **Datum:** UHSLC measures every level from its station zero (`reference_datum` "station zero"), named `ZERO` here, and gives no offsets to other datums, so `datums` is empty. A station UHSLC lists against any other reference gets that reference as its datum label, so the refresh opens or holds a datum epoch for it, and its levels are read under that label from then on; a row in any other reference than the station's is refused. A new RQ version under an FD series is a new benchmark: the FD series's `reference` is its RQ version letter, dated a day before the version's first daily value (the daily set can start a day after the hourly record). So when UHSLC moves a series to a new version, the next station refresh opens a datum epoch ([docs/stations.md](../../../docs/stations.md)) from the new version's start, closes the old one there, and a backfill fills the new version's history, rather than mixing two zeros in one file. The superseded version is then also listed as its own station, `uhslc/<number><version>`, so its values sit in that station and in the series's first epoch. The same happens if UHSLC drops a station from the FD set: the FD series goes inactive and its latest version is listed on its own.
- **Levels:** millimeters, as meters. Missing hours come as null and are dropped.
- **Flags:** ERDDAP's `quality` is 4 for RQ values, which are `good`, and 3 for FD values, which are `preliminary`. UHSLC documents no other values, so any other is `suspect`.
- **Hong Kong:** UHSLC stamps its Hong Kong hourly heights (Quarry Bay, station 329, both RQ versions and the FD series) an hour early: the height labeled T was measured at T + 1 h UTC, as tide-database found against the Hong Kong Observatory's predictions. The adapter moves those times an hour later (`EARLY_HOURS`) and asks for the hour before. Take it out once UHSLC corrects its files: the clock check below shows −60 min for UHSLC's times as they are and 0 with the correction, and would show +60 with the correction once UHSLC's times are right.
- **Clock check:** `scripts/clock-check.ts` cross-correlates a year of a series against a TICON record's prediction at 10-minute lags. On 2020 (2015 for the two Taiwanese gauges, and 1980 and 1970 for `uhslc/329a` and `uhslc/001a`), every series checked fits best within 10 minutes of zero against UHSLC's own TICON records: Pohnpei, Honolulu, Wellington, Port Louis, Fremantle, Hong Kong, Kaohsiung, Keelung, Abashiri, Lautoka and Vardø. Those constants were fitted to these records, so they only show a clock that changed since; against other originators' TICON records for the same gauges (BOM at Pohnpei, Fremantle and Lautoka, NOAA at Honolulu, Toitū Te Whenua at Wellington, JCG at Naze, Kartverket at Vardø), every one fits within 10 minutes too. Hong Kong's uncorrected times fit best at −60 min.
- **Limits:** ERDDAP publishes none. Requests start at least a second apart. A year of one station is about 300 KB and takes 1 to 4 seconds. A query with no matching rows answers 404 with "Your query produced no matching results" in the body, which is read as no values; any other 404, such as a renamed dataset, fails. The hourly RQ set can't give every series's first and last time within ERDDAP's 60-second gateway timeout, so the station refresh takes them from the daily sets (`global_daily_rqds`, `global_daily_fast`), and backfill asks only for the years from a month before a station's first day to a month after its last. An FD series still reporting has no last day (`record_end` is empty), since it would change on every refresh; one that has stopped for a year keeps its last day. A version the daily set doesn't list yet stops the refresh, which keeps the previous list, since an undated version would open its epoch at the refresh rather than at its start.
- **Freshness:** the station lists are never cached. Only an RQ version's responses are cached (`uhslc/<number><version>`), and only when every value is RQ and the year runs to its last hour, or to the version's last day. The FD series's responses never are: FD values change, and a new RQ version replaces even its RQ years under the same URL. A version's year cut at its last day is asked for with that day in the URL, so a release that extends it is fetched anew. UHSLC can still reissue a version's values in place, which only clearing `.cache/` picks up.

## Backfill

The restricted lake must be set, or ingest refuses:

```bash
WATER_LEVELS_RESTRICTED_LAKE=<private lake> node scripts/ingest.ts stations uhslc
```

```bash
WATER_LEVELS_RESTRICTED_LAKE=<private lake> node scripts/ingest.ts backfill uhslc all 1846 2026 --skip-written --first docs/ticon-replacement.csv
```

That's about 21,000 station-years. Years outside each station's record aren't asked for. It ends before the current year, which is fetched whole on every run and so is backfilled on its own, `backfill uhslc all 2026 2027`. Years with FD values hold preliminary values, and once ended `--skip-written` skips them, so a run without it fetches them again as RQ replaces FD. The Ingest and Monthly workflows don't run UHSLC yet, since neither has a restricted lake.
