# REFMAR (`refmar`)

Observed sea levels at about 170 French tide gauges, from SHOM's tide gauge service behind [data.shom.fr](https://data.shom.fr/), which serves the gauges [REFMAR](https://refmar.shom.fr/) coordinates: SHOM's own network (RONIM) and its partners' (ports, Vigicrues, local authorities, UHSLC), in metropolitan France and overseas. Brest goes back to 1846, Marseille to 1849.

- **License:** SHOM publishes both sets, [RONIM](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/reseaux-de-reference-des-observations-maregraphiques-refmar-maregraphes-ronim) and [partner gauges](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/reseaux-de-reference-des-observations-maregraphiques-refmar-maregraphes-partenaires), under the [Licence Ouverte 2.0](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/) (Etalab), which allows redistribution and commercial use. The datasets' ISO metadata (in SHOM's catalogue and [Sextant](https://sextant.ifremer.fr/geonetwork/api/collections/SIMM/items/OBSERVATION_NIVEAU_MER_REFMAR_PARTENAIRES.xml)) say the same, with no restriction on public access, and ask users to cite "REFMAR. http://dx.doi.org/10.17183/REFMAR#RONIM" (or `#PARTENAIRES`) and the date of the last update. So `redistribution` and `commercial_use` are true and every station goes to the public lake. Each station's attribution names the gauge's owners, as the service lists them, and its network's DOI. refmar.shom.fr's [Politique de diffusion](https://refmar.shom.fr/politique-d-acces-aux-donnees) still carries an older text, the REFMAR® production system's terms of use, which asks users to register and not to pass the data on, and limits free use to state services, research and private use. That text predates SHOM's move of REFMAR's downloads to data.shom.fr under the open licence (SHOM's 2015 annual report: "Les observations de hauteurs d'eau sont disponibles en licence ouverte sur data.shom.fr"), and the published datasets' licence is what governs the data served there.
- **Contact for errors:** refmar@shom.fr, where the [data access policy](https://refmar.shom.fr/politique-d-acces-aux-donnees) asks users to report questionable observations, or the [contact form](https://refmar.shom.fr/contact).
- **Withheld gauges:** the service's list marks three gauges `HIDDEN`, and any state but `OK`, `ASYNC`, `OLD`, `KO` and `PB` is treated the same way (Bassins à flot in Bordeaux, Saint-Nazaire GPMNSN and Toulon HTMNET), which data.shom.fr's map doesn't show. The service still answers for them, but SHOM doesn't publish them. The adapter lists them with no products and no datum, so a hidden gauge new to the list isn't added, and one already in the lake that SHOM hides later keeps what it has but is never fetched again: backfill, the refetch and `recent` all skip a station without levels, even one a backfill names, and the adapter refuses to fetch one. Its files already in the lake stay there.
- **Stations:** `GET /maregraphie/service/tidegauges` lists every gauge, and `GET /maregraphie/service/completetidegauge/<id>` gives each one's owners (`organismes`), vertical reference, geographic sector, time zone and first observation (`date_prem_obs`, missing at 39 gauges). IDs are SHOM's (`refmar/3` is Brest), as TICON's REFMAR records carry them (`brest-3-fra-refmar`). A gauge marked `OLD` has stopped reporting but its record is still served, so it stays active for backfill, with `live` false. Country codes come from SHOM's sector, with the Antilles–Guyane and Indian Ocean sectors split by position into GP, MQ, GF, MF and BL, and YT, RE and TF. Clipperton has no sector and no country code. `kind` isn't set.
- **Datum:** levels are in meters above the gauge's `zero_hydro`: chart datum (`zero_hydrographique`, `ZH`) at about 106 gauges, and an unknown instrument zero (`zero_instrumental_inconnu` or `non_rattaché`, `ZI`) at the rest, including the seven whose vertical reference names no zero; a gauge already in the lake with a named zero keeps it when its reference comes back empty. A zero the adapter doesn't know lists no datum, so the station keeps the one it has rather than opening a datum epoch. A record that fails, or comes back without a vertical reference or owners, leaves a gauge already in the lake as it was (owners' credit included), and keeps a new gauge out until the next refresh; a refresh doesn't fail on one gauge's record, but does when more than half the records asked for come back without one, since that's the service or a changed record shape rather than those gauges. Where SHOM gives chart datum's height in a land survey datum (`zh_ref` in `nom_ref`, such as IGN69 −3.635 m at Brest), that's the station's offset to it. The observations don't say their datum, so the adapter can't refuse values in another; a gauge SHOM ties to chart datum later opens a datum epoch at the next refresh.
- **Levels:** `GET /maregraphie/observation/json/<id>?sources=…&dtStart=…&dtEnd=…`, with both ends inclusive and in UTC. The service answers at most a month from `dtStart`, so the adapter asks a calendar month at a time. It has four series (`idsource`): 1, raw at 1 minute; 2, raw at 10 minutes; 3, SHOM's validated 10-minute values; 4, validated hourly values, filtered from those (and the only values for older years). For each month the adapter asks for series 2, 3 and 4 at once and takes the validated values (10-minute where both have a value), flagged `good`, then raw 10-minute values after the last validated one, flagged `preliminary`. Where that leaves more than the last value's own interval (an hour after an hourly value, ten minutes after any other) before the month's end, it asks for series 1 and takes its values on each ten minutes after that, also `preliminary`; it doesn't for months before 2005, before any gauge had 1-minute values. Raw values between two validated values of the whole range asked for are dropped too, so validation's gaps aren't refilled with the raw values it rejected, even a gap that runs to a month's end; raw values before the range's first validated value are kept, since they can be from before SHOM validated the gauge at all, unless the month before the range has validated values, in which case they're a rejected stretch too (within one month, raw values before its validated ones are always dropped), and where the range ends in raw values more than a week back, the month after it is asked for validated values, so a gap at the end of a backfill's year is dropped too. Only a whole month validation rejected outright, with raw values hourly ingest stored before, stays as it was: a fetch that gives a month nothing keeps what the lake has. Partner gauges often have no validated values for recent years, and only 1-minute raw values. Validation runs about two weeks behind at Brest, and raw 10-minute values about a day.
- **Time:** every gauge's `fuseau_horaire` is 0, and the service's timestamps are UTC (format notes: "la date et l'heure de la donnée en UTC").
- **Limits:** none published. Requests start at least half a second apart. A month of 10-minute values is about 50 KB compressed. An answer whose validated values run to its last hour is cached; any other isn't. A month before a gauge's `date_prem_obs`, less a month's margin, isn't asked for, nor anything after now. For the 39 gauges without one, a probe of 1 June every five years since 1960 found each one's first values, and the adapter starts five years before that (`UNDATED_FROM`; Pauillac from 1970, six more before 2005), or at 2010 for a gauge whose values the probe found from 2015 on or not at all. A gauge whose record starts before that loses those years.
- **Clock check:** on 2023, Brest, Nouméa, Papeete, Dzaoudzi and Fort-de-France fit UHSLC's records of the same gauges best at 0 minutes, and Marseille at 10 (its tide is small, r 0.59); K1's phase puts all six within 17 minutes, Nouméa (UTC+11) and Papeete (UTC−10) included. Pointe-à-Pitre (`refmar/125`) fits within 10 minutes against UHSLC's record in 2015, 2018, 2021 and 2022, but 60 to 70 minutes late in 2023 against UHSLC's, CMEMS's and REFMAR's own TICON records, and hardly at all in 2020 (r 0.36) and 2024 (r 0.15). That isn't one steady offset the adapter could correct, so erratum `refmar-pointe-a-pitre-clock` in [errata.json](errata.json) flags the gauge's values from 2020 through 2024 `suspect`: they stay in the lake, and fits and products skip them. SHOM's own flag stays in `source_flag`, so a validated value still counts as validated. 2025 fits within 10 minutes again (r 0.93), so the span ends with 2024. Once SHOM corrects the series, remove the entry locally, rerun the clock check on 2020 to 2024 (it skips `suspect` values, so it can't check them with the entry in place), then take the entry out and backfill the gauge's 2020 to 2025 with `--refetch`, since a plain backfill counts those years as done.

## Backfill

The Monthly workflow refreshes REFMAR's list on the 2nd with every other source's. The first list has to be in the lake before anything else runs: dispatch Monthly with `sources: refmar` once, or Ingest hourly, Compact daily and the refetch fail with no list. Then backfill through the Ingest workflow:

```bash
node scripts/ingest.ts backfill refmar all 1821 2026 --skip-written --first docs/ticon-replacement.csv
```

It starts at 1821, where TICON's Saint-Nazaire record does; a month before a gauge's first observation isn't asked for, so the early start costs nothing at gauges dated later. `date_prem_obs` puts about 66,000 station-months in the record of the 134 gauges that have one, and the 39 without one add about 9,500 from their starting years. Each month takes one request, or two from 2005 on where it isn't validated to its end, at most two a second, so the whole record is about 80,000 to 90,000 requests, 12 hours or more, likely longer where answers take more than half a second: three or four Ingest runs, each dispatched with `timeout` at 360 minutes and resuming where `--skip-written` finds the last one stopped. A year with no values writes no file, so each run asks again for the empty years between a gauge's starting year and its first values, and after an `OLD` gauge's last, a few thousand requests a run. `--first` puts the 106 gauges TICON has a REFMAR record for first. The current year is fetched whole on every run, so it's backfilled on its own, `backfill refmar all 2026 2027`. From then on Ingest hourly keeps the live gauges' raw values, and Monthly's refetch (`backfill refmar preliminary`) replaces them with validated ones as SHOM releases them. Like USACE's, it passes `--skip-unscreened` but in January, leaving out gauges with a year of record and no validated value, so the partner gauges SHOM doesn't validate aren't read again every month.
