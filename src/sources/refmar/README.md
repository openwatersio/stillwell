# REFMAR (`refmar`)

Observed sea levels at French tide gauges, in metropolitan France and overseas, from SHOM's tide gauge service behind [data.shom.fr](https://data.shom.fr/). It serves the gauges [REFMAR](https://refmar.shom.fr/) coordinates: SHOM's own network (RONIM) and its partners' (ports, Vigicrues, local authorities, UHSLC).

- **Stations:** every gauge in the service's list. IDs are SHOM's (`refmar/3` is Brest), the ones TICON's REFMAR records carry. Each station's credit names the gauge's owners, as the service lists them, and its network's DOI. A gauge SHOM marks `OLD` has stopped reporting, but its record is still served, so it stays active with `live` false. Country codes come from SHOM's sector, with the Antilles–Guyane and Indian Ocean sectors split by position. Clipperton has no sector and no country code. The service doesn't say what water a gauge measures, so `kind` is null except where a decision in `KINDS` ([`src/kinds.ts`](../../kinds.ts)) sets it.
- **Withheld gauges:** SHOM marks some gauges `HIDDEN` and leaves them off data.shom.fr's map, such as Bassins à flot in Bordeaux, Saint-Nazaire GPMNSN and Toulon HTMNET. The store treats any state but `OK`, `ASYNC`, `OLD`, `KO` and `PB` the same way. A hidden gauge new to the list isn't added. One already in the store keeps the values it has but gets no new ones.
- **Levels:** the service has four series (`idsource`): 1, raw at 1 minute; 2, raw at 10 minutes; 3, SHOM's validated 10-minute values; 4, validated hourly values filtered from those, and the only values for older years. For each month the store takes the validated values, 10-minute where both have one. After the last validated value it takes raw 10-minute values. Where those stop short of the month's end, it takes series 1's values on each ten minutes after that, from 2005 on. Raw values between two validated values are dropped, so a gap validation left isn't refilled with the raw values it rejected. Raw values from before SHOM validated the gauge at all are kept. A whole month validation rejected outright keeps the raw values the poller stored before. Partner gauges often have no validated values for recent years, and only 1-minute raw values.
- **History:** a gauge's record starts a month before its first observation (`date_prem_obs`). For a gauge without one, it starts five years before the first values a probe of every fifth year found, or at 2010 where the probe found values only from 2015 on or none.
- **Datum:** levels are in meters above the gauge's `zero_hydro`: chart datum (`ZH`, `zero_hydrographique`) or an unknown instrument zero (`ZI`, `zero_instrumental_inconnu` or `non_rattaché`, or a vertical reference that names no zero). Where SHOM gives chart datum's height in a land survey datum (`zh_ref` in `nom_ref`, such as IGN69 −3.635 m at Brest), that's the station's offset to it. A gauge with a named zero keeps it when its reference comes back empty, and a zero the adapter doesn't know leaves the station's datum as it was. The observations don't say their datum, so the adapter can't refuse values in another. A gauge SHOM ties to chart datum later opens a datum epoch at the next refresh.
- **Times:** UTC. Every gauge's `fuseau_horaire` is 0, and the service documents its timestamps as UTC ("la date et l'heure de la donnée en UTC"). Brest, Nouméa, Papeete, Dzaoudzi, Fort-de-France and Marseille match UHSLC's records of the same gauges within 10 minutes, Nouméa (UTC+11) and Papeete (UTC−10) included. Pointe-à-Pitre's clock (erratum `refmar-pointe-a-pitre-clock`) and Kerguelen's raw series (erratum `refmar-kerguelen-raw-hour-late`) are exceptions.
- **Flags:** a value's flag comes from its series. Validated values are `good` and raw values `preliminary`. SHOM replaces raw values with validated ones as it releases them.
- **Freshness:** raw 10-minute values arrive about a day behind, and the poller keeps the live gauges' raw values. Validation runs about two weeks behind at Brest. A partner gauge with a year of record and no validated value, which SHOM doesn't validate, is read for validated values only in January.

## Known limits

- **Undated gauges:** a gauge with no first observation whose record starts before its probed start year is missing those years.

## Running ingest

- **Withheld gauges:** backfill, the refetch and the poller all skip a station without levels, even one a backfill names, and the adapter refuses to fetch one.
- **Stations:** `GET /maregraphie/service/tidegauges` lists every gauge, and `GET /maregraphie/service/completetidegauge/<id>` gives each one's owners, vertical reference, geographic sector, time zone and first observation. A record that fails, or comes back without a vertical reference or owners, leaves a gauge already in the store as it was, owners' credit included, and keeps a new gauge out until the next refresh. A refresh doesn't fail on one gauge's record, but does when more than half the records asked for come back without one, since that's the service or a changed record shape rather than those gauges.
- **Levels:** `GET /maregraphie/observation/json/<id>?sources=…&dtStart=…&dtEnd=…`, with both ends inclusive and in UTC. The service answers at most a month from `dtStart`, so the adapter asks a calendar month at a time. For each month the adapter asks for series 2, 3 and 4 at once, and asks for series 1 only where the raw 10-minute values stop short of the month's end, never for months before 2005. Raw values between two validated values of the whole range asked for are dropped. Raw values before the range's first validated value are kept, unless the month before the range has validated values, in which case they're a rejected stretch too (within one month, raw values before its validated ones are always dropped). Where the range ends in raw values more than a week back, the month after it is asked for validated values, so a gap at the end of a backfill's year is dropped too. A fetch that gives a month nothing keeps what the store has.
- **Limits:** none published. Requests start at least half a second apart. A month of 10-minute values is about 50 KB compressed. An answer whose validated values run to its last hour is cached; any other isn't. A month before a gauge's `date_prem_obs`, less a month's margin, isn't asked for, nor anything in the future. The starting years for the gauges without one are `UNDATED_FROM` in [index.ts](index.ts).

### Backfill

The Monthly workflow refreshes REFMAR's list on the 2nd with every other source's. The first list has to be in the store before anything else runs: dispatch Monthly with `sources: refmar` once, or the poller, Compact daily and the refetch fail with no list. Then backfill through the Ingest workflow:

```bash
node scripts/ingest.ts backfill refmar all 1821 2026 --skip-written --first docs/ticon-inventory.csv
```

It starts at 1821, where TICON's Saint-Nazaire record does. A month before a gauge's first observation isn't asked for, so the early start costs nothing at gauges dated later. The record is about 75,000 station-months. Each month takes one request, or two from 2005 on where it isn't validated to its end, at most two a second, so the whole record is about 80,000 to 90,000 requests, 12 hours or more: three or four Ingest runs, each dispatched with `timeout` at 360 minutes and resuming where `--skip-written` finds the last one stopped. A year with no values writes no file, so each run asks again for the empty years between a gauge's starting year and its first values, and after an `OLD` gauge's last, a few thousand requests a run. `--first` puts the gauges TICON has a REFMAR record for first. The current year is fetched whole on every run, so it's backfilled on its own, `backfill refmar all 2026 2027`.

### Scheduled runs

The poller keeps the live gauges' raw values. The source's `finalizes` is true, so Monthly's refetch (`backfill refmar preliminary`) replaces raw values with validated ones as SHOM releases them. Like USACE's, it passes `--skip-unscreened` but in January, leaving out gauges with a year of record and no validated value, so the partner gauges SHOM doesn't validate aren't read again every month.
