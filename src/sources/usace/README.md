# USACE CWMS (`usace`)

Stage from the US Army Corps of Engineers' Corps Water Management System, read through the public [CWMS Data API](https://cwms-data.usace.army.mil/cwms-data/) (CDA; [docs](https://cwms-data-api.readthedocs.io/), [source](https://github.com/USACE/cwms-data-api)). It covers the district offices on the Mississippi, Ohio, Missouri and their tributaries: MVS, MVK, MVM, MVN, LRL, LRP, LRH, LRN and LRC.

- **Stations:** one per CWMS location with an observed `Stage.Inst` series. Most are river gauges, but the list also has reservoir pools, lock and dam headwaters and tailwaters, and the riverside and landside of pump stations. Piezometers and observation wells, which read groundwater in a dam's foundation, aren't listed. The ID is `<office>.<location>`, such as `usace/MVN.Baton_Rouge`, with anything but letters, digits, `.`, `_` and `-` replaced by `_`. Some gauges appear in two offices' databases under different names, such as `MVN.Baton_Rouge` and `MVK.Baton_Rouge`, and each is listed.
- **Kind:** `river` where CWMS's location kind or the district's type says stream, or the location is a run-of-river dam, whose pool is the river. `lake` where it's a project or reservoir, since a project's stage is its pool. A location named for a tailwater is never `lake`. Everything else has no `kind`: types such as DCP, Gage and Lock, no type at all, and pump stations, canals and lock sides.
- **Series:** a location usually has several, named `<location>.Stage.Inst.<interval>.<duration>.<version>`, and each office labels versions its own way. The store takes observed series, 5 minutes to a day apart, whose version ends in `rev` or `raw` or is `OBS`, `OBS-USGS`, `Observed`, `MVM-PUB` or `historic-data-merge`. It leaves out forecasts, statistics and versions that name a datum, which are the stage converted to that datum. Of a location's series, it takes the first that is still reporting, then finer than daily, then revised (`rev`) over `raw`, then the finest interval, then the longest record. So a daily series is taken only where nothing finer still reports. A station keeps its series while CWMS lists it, so its files never switch series, and it goes inactive when CWMS stops listing it.
- **Levels:** stage in meters, from CDA's SI units, rounded to the millimeter.
- **Datum:** `GZ`, the gauge's zero. Where CWMS gives the zero's elevation and names NGVD29 or NAVD88 as its vertical datum, `datums` carries it, so `level_m + datums.NGVD29` is the level above NGVD29. Local and unnamed datums give no offset, and nor does an elevation of exactly 0 (erratum `usace-zero-elevation`). The offsets at St. Louis and Vicksburg match USGS's gauges there. The Chicago Harbor Lock's two stations have no offsets, since CWMS gives their zero no elevation.
- **NAVD88 from the description:** the New Orleans District (MVN) states most gauges' adjustment to NAVD88 only in the location's description, such as "Adjustment for vertical datum NAVD88 (OPUS 2021): -1.403 ft". The store reads MVN's descriptions only. The adjustment is what to add to the stage, and goes in `datums.NAVD88` over any catalog elevation. Where a description gives several, the one with the latest date counts. Several that differ undated or on one day give none, and so does one dated before the gauge's last zero reset. Where none is stated, a gauge whose zero was reset to NAVD88 the day before its reset date in [`zeros.ts`](zeros.ts) gets 0. The store's list records each offset's words, the realization they name and their date as `datums_from`, such as `{"NAVD88": {"description": "Adjustment for vertical datum NAVD88 (OPUS 2021): -1.403 ft", "realization": "OPUS 2021", "as_of": null}}`. `stations.parquet` doesn't carry it.
- **LWRP:** some MVN descriptions give the offset from NAVD88 to the 2007 Low Water Reference Plane, which slopes along the river. `datums.LWRP` is the NAVD88 adjustment plus that offset, where both name the same realization or the LWRP sentence names none: the gauges from Knox Landing to Venice. Baton Rouge's two name different realizations, so it has none. The offsets match MVN's LWRP white paper at Knox Landing, Baton Rouge, New Orleans and Venice ([#10](https://github.com/openwatersio/stillwell/issues/10)).
- **Gauge zeros:** at about 30 stations, most in MVN, the gauge zero moved partway through the series, typically from NGVD29 to NAVD88. CWMS keeps one series across the reset and says so only in the description, so [`zeros.ts`](zeros.ts) gives each date with the description's words. Backfill never fetches a value from before the reset, so a station's files are on one zero and `datums` applies to all of them. Venice's stage drops by the step its description gives on its reset day.
- **Flags:** CWMS quality is a bit field, read as USACE's [CWMS-Data-Quality](https://github.com/USACE-WaterManagement/CWMS-Data-Quality) library reads it. Missing (4) and a null value are left out. Rejected (16) is `bad` and questionable (8) is `suspect`. Unscreened (quality 0) and screened without a verdict (quality 1) are `preliminary`: as the gauge reported them, and the Corps may revise them. A value replaced by linear interpolation is `interpolated`, and anything else screened okay is `good`. Some offices, such as St. Louis, screen nothing, so all their values are preliminary. MVN screens most of its values.
- **Quality control:** navigation pools sit within centimeters for months and then rise meters in a flood, so QC treats them as still water, with no outlier rule. Until a station's first analysis, or where its series is too coarse to judge, QC goes by `kind`, and a station with no `kind` is checked as a tide gauge. The gauges of coastal Louisiana measure as tidal: the Mississippi from New Orleans down, the Atchafalaya at Morgan City and the Calcasieu.
- **Freshness:** live stations are read hourly. Each month the store reads each station again from its earliest preliminary value. A gauge with a year of record and no screened value is read again only in January.

## Known limits

- **Subsidence:** a NAVD88 adjustment holds as of the survey it comes from, but the gauges subside and are re-surveyed, and the store applies one value to the whole record since the last zero reset. At some gauges the description's adjustment differs from the one MVN applies in its own `rev-NAVD88` series (erratum `usace-lwrp-corrections-disagree`).
- **Unmarked zero moves:** gauges that moved with no datum noted, as several in MVM did, and MVN's Head of Passes, a new gauge set to the old one's water surface, have no reset date, so their records can have steps.
- **Bad stretches in prose:** some descriptions note bad values only in prose, and those values keep CWMS's flags. Reserve's description says its values from 1991-10-04 to 1993-01-14 "are 1 foot too high", but its CWMS series begins in 1997, so the store holds none of them.
- **Later screening:** screening an office does on values more than 120 days old reaches the store only once the cache is cleared.

## Running ingest

- **Request limits:** none published and none measured. CDA sends no rate-limit headers, and a run of 40 one-year requests all answered 200, in 0.5 to 2 seconds each. Throttling (429) and server errors are retried with backoff, as for every source. Stay polite anyway: a backfill runs four stations at a time and each station's requests one after another.
- **Workflows:** The poller reads the last 12 hours at the live stations and Compact daily folds the last 6 days into the year files. The Monthly workflow refreshes the station list on the 2nd and re-reads each station from its earliest preliminary value on the 9th.
- **Refresh failures:** an office that answers with no series or no locations fails the refresh, and the store's list stays as it was. When CWMS stops listing a station's series, the refresh that marks the station inactive prints the series CWMS now offers, lists the station as retired by the source in its report, and exits nonzero, so the run fails. Left inactive, the station is retired; setting its `series` to the one CWMS now offers brings it back. `stations.parquet` doesn't carry `series`, so a station read from there takes it from `source_url`, which names it.
- **Refreshed offsets:** a refresh whose description no longer gives an offset says so. If the station then has no offsets at all, the stored ones and their `datums_from` are kept, as for any refresh that sends none, so taking back an offset read wrongly takes an edit of the store's list. If the catalog still gives an NGVD29 elevation, that replaces them.
- **Zero dates:** the refresh copies a station's [`zeros.ts`](zeros.ts) date onto it as `zero_since` and keeps the later of the two. Adding a station there later opens a datum epoch from its date ([when an epoch opens](https://openwatersio.github.io/stillwell/docs/guides/datums/#when-an-epoch-opens)), so values already stored stay in epoch 1 and new ones go to files of their own.
- **Moved positions:** MVN's Baton Rouge sits at MVK's position, and a group holds one USACE station, so the publish report lists it as skipped.
- **Requests:** `timeseries` in SI units, a calendar year per request, following `next-page`. A response with values in anything but meters is refused. An empty range answers `"values": []`, before a series begins and after it ends alike, so a 200 with no `values` array, such as a maintenance page, fails and isn't cached. A series CWMS doesn't know answers 404. Values are UTC epoch milliseconds.
- **Unscreened gauges:** the Monthly refetch passes `--skip-unscreened` for USACE in every month but January, so a gauge with a year of record and no screened value isn't fetched again each month. January's run takes them all, so a station whose office has begun screening can rejoin.
- **Backfill size:** the refresh keeps the UTC day each station's series begins in CWMS's catalog as `series_since`, and backfill asks for nothing before January 1 of that year, so `backfill usace all` over a long range sends no request for the years before a series begins. A refresh keeps the earlier of the catalog's day and the one the list had. The catalog's end is left alone, since its extents lag the data by weeks, so a series that has ended is still asked for every year after it. A station whose series CWMS stops listing keeps the day its list entry had. One with no `series_since`, such as a station read from `stations.parquet` or one first listed with a series CWMS gives no extents for, is asked for every year it's given. The daily records reach back to 1861; shard long backfills by station and range.
- **Cache:** responses whose range ends within the last 120 days, and the catalogs, are never cached, since offices revise recent values. Older years are cached for good.
