# BSH tide predictions (`bsh`)

Predicted high and low waters and 10-minute curves for 172 German gauges from the [BSH tide service](https://gezeiten.bsh.de/), for the current year and, from about August, the next.

- **License:** free, including commercial use and publication, under Anlage 4 of BSH's [fee schedule for digital data](https://www.bsh.de/DE/Das_BSH/Gebuehren_Preise/Gebuehren_und_Preise/_Anlagen/Downloads/Entgeltverzeichnis-digitale-Daten.pdf). A year's predictions may be published online only from 1 August of the year before. The source notice is "Datenquelle: Gezeitenvorausberechnungen ©, Bundesamt für Seeschifffahrt und Hydrographie". See [slackwater-database#335](https://github.com/openwatersio/slackwater-database/issues/335).
- **Contact for errors:** gezeiten@bsh.de, the tide service's mailbox, given on [gezeiten.bsh.de](https://gezeiten.bsh.de/).
- **Stations:** the gauges in BSH's overview, with offsets from gauge zero (PNP) to NHN and to chart datum (SKN) from the latest year. Each year's file names its datum, PNP at most gauges and SKN at 10; a station's datum is the latest year's, and a backfill refuses a year in any other. Where BSH names a PEGELONLINE series for the gauge (103 of the 172), `same_as` links to it and observed levels come from there: Cuxhaven is `bsh/506P` for predictions and `pegelonline/5990020` for levels. `kind` is null: the overview gives only a name, number, position and an undocumented `gauge_group`, and only some names end in a water (", Elbe", ", Ostsee").
- **Refresh:** the station list refreshes on the 2nd, and the same job fetches the predictions (Schedule, below). BSH `finalizes` nothing and has no live feed, so neither Ingest hourly nor the Monthly refetch reads it.
- **Method:** high and low waters from the harmonic representation of inequalities, the curve between them from a mean observed tide curve. [docs/hroi-evaluation.md](../../../docs/hroi-evaluation.md) has more.
- **Times:** from each row's Julian date, which is UTC and finer than the minute-resolution local time beside it.
- **Schedule:** nothing hourly, since a year's predictions are published whole and not revised. Each month the Monthly workflow's BSH refresh also fetches this year's predictions for every gauge, and from August to December next year's too (`backfill bsh all <this year> <next year + 1>`), so a gauge added in the year, or a month whose run failed, is filled the next month. The adapter asks for no year before 1 August of the year before, when BSH's terms let it be published, whoever runs the backfill. A year BSH hasn't published yet answers 404 and writes nothing, and an unchanged year rewrites no file. BSH's files run by German local time, so the last hour of each UTC year sits in the next year's file, and the store doesn't have it. A gauge's `first` and `last` describe observed levels, which come from PEGELONLINE, so they're null here.
- **Extremes** carry BSH's lunar transit number and event index (1 high water after upper transit, 2 low water after upper, 3 high after lower, 4 low after lower).
