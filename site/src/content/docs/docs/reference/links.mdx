`stations/links.parquet` holds offsets measured between the levels of two linked stations, not the links themselves, which are `same_as` in `stations.parquet` (see [Gauges](/docs/reference/gauges/)). It has a row for each pair of stations of one gauge within 1 km of each other that share no reference datum, where one has MSL, LAT or a chart datum of its own (not `computed`) that the other lacks. Publish carries those datums across the pair by the measured offset, which is how a PEGELONLINE gauge gets MSL and LAT from a GESLA file of it, and that GESLA file gets SKN back. [Datums](/docs/reference/datums/#where-offsets-come-from) describes the other ways offsets are carried.

    offset_m:

    spread_m:
      "How far the medians of the measured months range, in meters, over months with at least 10 days.",
  }}
/>

## How a pair is measured

The Monthly workflow measures each pair in the latest calendar year of their open epochs that measures. It tries up to three of the years in which both stations' files hold at least 30 days, latest first. At each minute both stations have a good or preliminary value, it takes the first such minute in each hour, and `offset_m` is the median of the differences over those hours. A pair needs at least 30 days of hours and two months with at least 10 days each.

`spread_m` shows whether the offset holds. Two series of one gauge differ by a constant, so their monthly medians agree. A datum step inside the year, or two gauges whose levels part with a river's flow, spreads wider.

## When publish uses a pair

Publish carries offsets along a pair whose `spread_m` is 1 cm or less and whose `a_epoch` and `b_epoch` are still the stations' open epochs. A station that opens a new epoch waits for the next month's measurement. Publish also leaves out a row whose stations have since moved more than 1 km apart, and fails if it can't read the file, rather than publish without the offsets it gives.

A row the month's run doesn't replace is kept, so a missing file, a rewrite in progress or a failed read takes nothing away. A pair whose values were read but don't measure, or that is no longer linked, loses its row.

## Terms

The rows are derived from both stations' levels and carry no terms of their own. Join them to `stations.parquet` for each station's `commercial_use` and `attribution`.

## Examples

A gauge's measured pairs:

<QueryResult
  sql={`SET TimeZone = 'UTC';
SELECT a, b, since::DATE AS since, until::DATE AS until, hours, offset_m, spread_m
FROM 'https://stillwell.openwaters.io/stations/links.parquet'
WHERE a = 'pegelonline/5990020' OR b = 'pegelonline/5990020'
ORDER BY a, b`}
/>

How many pairs each pair of sources has, and how many publish can use:

<QueryResult
  sql={`SELECT split_part(a, '/', 1) AS source_a, split_part(b, '/', 1) AS source_b,
  count(*) AS pairs, count(*) FILTER (spread_m <= 0.01) AS within_1_cm
FROM 'https://stillwell.openwaters.io/stations/links.parquet'
GROUP BY ALL ORDER BY pairs DESC LIMIT 10`}
/>

The pairs that spread widest, with both stations' terms:

<QueryResult
  sql={`SELECT l.a, l.b, l.offset_m, l.spread_m, sa.commercial_use AS a_commercial, sb.commercial_use AS b_commercial
FROM 'https://stillwell.openwaters.io/stations/links.parquet' l
JOIN 'https://stillwell.openwaters.io/stations.parquet' sa ON sa.id = l.a
JOIN 'https://stillwell.openwaters.io/stations.parquet' sb ON sb.id = l.b
ORDER BY l.spread_m DESC LIMIT 5`}
/>
