A station is one source's series at one place. `stations.parquet` has a row for every station in the store, active or not: where it is, what it measures from, which products it has, how far back its record goes, whether it carries a tide, and the terms its data comes under. It's the place to start any question, since every other file is named by a station's `id`.

A station isn't always a gauge of its own. Cuxhaven, for example, is `pegelonline/5990020` for observed levels and `bsh/506P` for predictions, and GESLA and UHSLC hold copies of it too. [Gauges](/docs/reference/gauges/) describes how those stations link to each other.

`stations.parquet` is rebuilt after compaction each night, so its record spans usually trail the store by about a day.

## Status and polling

A station stays in the store when its source stops listing it: it becomes `inactive`, and its history stays. A source can also list a station as ended, such as a CO-OPS station with a `removed` date that's off the current network, and its adapter marks it inactive. `live` stations are polled every hour into `recent/<source>.parquet`; the rest arrive with backfills and the monthly refetch. `min_interval_s` is the source's own limit on how often a station may be asked for, where it states one.

## Record

`first` and `last` bound the station's observations over all its datum epochs, from what compaction and backfill write. They're null for a station with no observations yet, which happens while a source's history is still being backfilled. The years a station actually has, and how many values each holds by flag, are in its source's years list: see [Series](/docs/reference/series/#which-years-a-station-has).

## Where the source checks its values

`source_qc_start` is where the source's quality checks of the station's values start. A source's `preliminary` values before it won't be checked, so they won't change. Null means unknown: it doesn't mean the source never checks the station's values, or that it has checked them all. `source_qc_start_inferred` says whether Stillwell worked the date out from the source's flags or the source stated it; add `source_qc_start_inferred = false` to keep to stated dates. Each source's README says where its dates come from.

## Tide

`tidal` says whether a station's water carries a tide, measured from its levels rather than taken from `kind`: a river can be tidal, and the Baltic isn't. It's true where M2's and O1's amplitudes, fitted over the station's last year of usable values and added as `tide_m`, come to 8 cm or more, with exceptions for water well above sea level and for creeks that drain to a sill. Both are null where the values span under 30 days or are sampled too coarsely to fit, and before the station's first analysis. Stillwell's quality checks for flatlines, outliers, held readings and jumps away from the tide follow `tidal`.

## Terms

Each station carries its terms: `license` is its source's, `commercial_use` says whether those terms allow commercial use, and `attribution` is the credit to show wherever its data is shown. Where a contributor's own terms apply, as at some UHSLC and GESLA records, `commercial_use` follows them and `attribution` adds the contributor's credit. Credit the offsets that don't come from the source too: they're in `datums_attribution`.

## Examples

How many stations each source has, and in which datums:

<QueryResult
  sql={`SELECT source, count(*) AS stations, string_agg(DISTINCT datum, ', ' ORDER BY datum) AS datums
FROM 'https://stillwell.openwaters.io/stations.parquet'
WHERE status = 'active'
GROUP BY source ORDER BY source`}
/>

A station by name:

<QueryResult
  sql={`SELECT id, name, kind, status, first, last
FROM 'https://stillwell.openwaters.io/stations.parquet'
WHERE name ILIKE '%hoek van holland%'
ORDER BY id`}
/>

The nearest active stations with observed levels to a point, here the mouth of the Nieuwe Waterweg, one per gauge:

<QueryResult
  sql={`SELECT id, name,
  round(6371 * acos(least(1,
    sin(radians(lat)) * sin(radians(51.98)) +
    cos(radians(lat)) * cos(radians(51.98)) * cos(radians(lon - 4.12)))), 2) AS km
FROM 'https://stillwell.openwaters.io/stations.parquet'
WHERE status = 'active' AND list_contains(products, 'levels') AND preferred = id
ORDER BY km LIMIT 5`}
/>

Tidal stations whose terms allow commercial use, by source:

<QueryResult
  sql={`SELECT source, count(*) AS stations, round(median(tide_m), 2) AS median_tide_m
FROM 'https://stillwell.openwaters.io/stations.parquet'
WHERE tidal AND commercial_use AND status = 'active'
GROUP BY source ORDER BY stations DESC`}
/>

Where sources start checking their values, for the stations that have a date:

<QueryResult
  sql={`SELECT source, count(*) AS stations, min(source_qc_start)::DATE AS earliest,
  max(source_qc_start)::DATE AS latest, bool_or(source_qc_start_inferred) AS any_inferred
FROM 'https://stillwell.openwaters.io/stations.parquet'
WHERE source_qc_start IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY source ORDER BY source`}
/>
