# GESLA contributors

Generated by `node scripts/gesla-contributors.ts` from GESLA 4.1's metadata, 6682 records. Every record GESLA serves is in the public lake, credited to GESLA and its contributor: GESLA serves them all openly, so under the lake's rule each goes in with its contributor's terms tagged, and only data a source itself withholds stays out. Where a contributor's own terms ask for permission to republish, it's being asked for: JODC ([#47](https://github.com/openwatersio/water-levels/issues/47)), UNAM ([#48](https://github.com/openwatersio/water-levels/issues/48)), BODC, BOM and DMI ([#80](https://github.com/openwatersio/water-levels/issues/80)) and SANHO ([#82](https://github.com/openwatersio/water-levels/issues/82)). GESLA's [license page](https://gesla787883612.wordpress.com/license/) says which contributors' data may be used for consultancy as well as research; a record's `commercial_use` is the stricter of that and the originator's own terms where they're known, and [src/sources/gesla/contributors.ts](../src/sources/gesla/contributors.ts) holds each contributor's. UHSLC's records take each record's terms from UHSLC's documentation files ([uhslc-contributors.md](uhslc-contributors.md)), and its South African records are the South African Navy Hydrographic Office's, non-commercial while SANHO is asked for permission to republish them ([#82](https://github.com/openwatersio/water-levels/issues/82)). "Originator ID" counts the records whose site code gives an ID in the originator's lake source, whether or not that source lists it; `stations.parquet` links others by nearness and name.
