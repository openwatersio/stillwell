## Claims from snippets, unverified

- The stage field manual describes a gauge correction applied to recorded levels for a vertical datum change, a sensor reset correction for large sensor drift, and reference water level readings used to check the sensor and to fill gaps. The levelling manual reportedly says a gauge correction is applied to all stage data when the closure discrepancy exceeds 0.003 m.
- The Data Computations manual reportedly covers shifts that vary with stage or time, and warns that automated deletion of stage series in the Data Correction Toolbox can create a blended rating and change approved data.
- The Water Survey real-time site distinguishes Provisional and approved or final data. It warns that ice near a station invalidates the stage-discharge relationship, and that water level on the site may differ from the actual ice or water level at the gauge. The qualifier table was not captured.
