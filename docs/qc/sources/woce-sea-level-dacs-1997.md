## What it describes

The WOCE split that still shapes GLOSS: UHSLC as the "fast delivery" Data Assembly Centre (data within 1-3 months, for altimetry checks) and BODC as the "delayed mode" DAC (full QC, 18-24 months). WOCE requirements: elevations accurate to 1 cm, timing to 2 minutes, atmospheric pressure to 1 mbar.

## Delayed-mode QC at BODC

- QC is done on at least a year of data, because "it is easier to detect and correct datum shifts using a longer time series and the effort required to check 1 year of data is similar to that required for 1 month of data."
- "There are three main components to the sea level data quality assurance: flagging obvious wrong values, correcting timing drifts and maintaining reference level stability."
- Values are checked for "spikes and physically unreasonable values"; tidal analysis of each year (Doodson method), constituents compared with previous years and constituent banks; "The residuals produced from tidal analysis are screened to check for datum shifts, timing problems and other errors." Adjacent stations and other parameters (atmospheric pressure, sea temperature) are plotted together.
- Datum shifts are referred to the supplier; unresolved shifts are documented and records are split into separate files where reference levels are not linked by levelling to the same benchmarks.
- A quality assessment reports the proportion of data without "questionable fluctuations in the residuals"; each case is checked as natural event, mechanical problem, or unreliable predictions. Site peculiarities to document include "complex local geography, seiching, silting up of the harbour, river mouths".
- Gaps are filled from redundant sensors where possible; otherwise flagged as null and documented.

## Fast delivery at UHSLC

QC "essentially parallel[s]" delayed mode, emphasizing redundant data sources (for example tsunami warning center sensors) "to fill gaps and recognize and correct level shifts", fast feedback to originators, and a "fully corrected" data set: "erroneous or questionable data are not only flagged, but every effort is made to correct them".
