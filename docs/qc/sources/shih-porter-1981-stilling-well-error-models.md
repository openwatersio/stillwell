# Error Models for Stilling Well - Float Type Tide Gages

## Citation

Shih, H. H. and Porter, D. L. Error Models for Stilling Well - Float Type Tide Gages. In Proceedings of OCEANS 81, Boston, 16-18 September 1981, pp. 1118-1123.

## What it says

The conference version of [shih-rogers-1981-stilling-well-errors.md](shih-rogers-1981-stilling-well-errors.md): the same error models for wind waves, currents, float response, density variation and marine fouling in float gauges with stilling wells (abstract, p. 1118). It adds laboratory validation: a wave tank for the level's response to waves, a tow tank for drawdown from currents, and an oscillatory water tunnel for the float's response, with details left to a separate engineering report (section 4, p. 1120). It concludes that the validated models could evaluate existing and new station designs and the sensitivity of tidal datums to measurement error (section 5, p. 1120).

## Relevance to Stillwell

As for the full report: the errors are biases that only a comparison with another gauge finds.
