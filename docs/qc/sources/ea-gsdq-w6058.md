## What it is

A scoring system that grades a gauging station's data quality, not a procedure for validating individual values. It is included as the closest primary document found from the Environment Agency; the Agency's operational hydrometric validation procedure was not found. It says it can be used to set data targets and give users information on quality.

## Level-only scoring scheme (6.7, pp. 58 to 59; 7.13, p. 62 to 63)

Stations that only record level are scored on eight attributes with weights: type of instrument (2), non-capture rate as percent missing data (0.8), accuracy of level measurement in mm (1.2), average annual number of manual checks of level (1.2), truncation of measured level at high flows (0.4), truncation at low flows (0.4), weed growth management (1.2), and siltation management of the intake pipe and stilling well (0.8). The scheme "is focussed on the effective accuracy of stage measurement." Other schemes (rated sections, electromagnetic, and so on) rely mainly on stage-discharge uncertainty and gaugings.

## Other EA material seen but not reviewed

An Agrimetrics support page (https://dsp.agrimetrics.co.uk/support/faqs/275875350/275875565) says EA data are checked for plausibility by a hydrometric expert after a delay and given quality flags, that recent telemetered data are not quality assured, and that logged values drift and are recalibrated against manual dips. This was read only as a search-result snippet, not verified against the EA's own documentation. The EA hydrometric data feed manual (https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7cde67ed915d71e1e4e203/Hydrometric_data_feed_User_Manual_v1.0.pdf) was not opened.

## Not covered

No range, rate-of-change, spike, flat-line or datum thresholds.
