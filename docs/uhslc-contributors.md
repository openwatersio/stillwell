# UHSLC contributors

Generated by `node scripts/uhslc-contributors.ts` from UHSLC's research-quality documentation files (`https://uhslc.soest.hawaii.edu/rqds/<ocean>/doc/qa<id>.dmt`), 747 records. Every station UHSLC serves openly is in the public lake under UHSLC's terms ("may be used and redistributed for free but is not intended for legal use"). Contributors whose own terms are stricter have been asked for permission to republish. The South African Navy Hydrographic Office refused ([#80](https://github.com/openwatersio/water-levels/issues/80), [#82](https://github.com/openwatersio/water-levels/issues/82)), so every series of a station number one of whose files carries its notice is withheld from the lake, along with Lüderitz (702), whose file names no contributor but which GESLA credits to SANHO (`ALSO_WITHHELD` in notices.ts), and GESLA's copies of them; the other contributors' stations stay in it, under their own terms, while they answer. Where a contributor's file carries terms of its own, the station's `commercial_use` follows them and its `attribution` adds the contributor's credit; [src/sources/uhslc/notices.ts](../src/sources/uhslc/notices.ts) holds those terms. The fast-delivery series take their latest research-quality version's file; those with no research-quality data have no file, and nor do a few research-quality records, so their contributor is unknown and UHSLC's terms apply. A record released since the last scan is tagged non-commercial until a rescan reads it.
