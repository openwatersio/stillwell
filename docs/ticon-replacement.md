Generated by `npm run ticon:inventory` from tide-database at `64713d1a1` and the station lists in the store; don't edit by hand. [ticon-replacement.csv](ticon-replacement.csv) has one row per TICON record. This is phase 2 of [plan.md](plan.md).

The status is the originator's: the aim is an adapter for every source in GESLA, which brings live values and years after GESLA's release. Meanwhile 2457 of the 2527 accepted records have a GESLA 4.1 copy listed in the store (`gesla_id` in the CSV; its backfill is still running), which can stand in for the originator in a re-fit. `gesla_commercial_use` gives the copy's terms as the `gesla` source tags them, from GESLA's table and its contributor's terms where this repo knows them; where the originator's own terms are still unconfirmed (`license`), take the stricter until they are. GESLA 4.1 renamed or dropped RWS's records, so this name match finds no RWS copy; [the GESLA adapter's README](../src/sources/gesla/README.md) maps some of the renamed ones by hand.

## How records are matched

- **Codes in the id.** TICON ids end in `<code>-<country>-<contributor>`. NOAA, USGS, REFMAR, Norwegian (`nhs`) and SMHI records carry the originator's station code, so they map to `coops`, `usgs` (with the leading zero TICON drops restored, to USGS's 8, 10 or 15 digits, or to 9 where USGS lists that site), `kartverket`, `refmar` and `smhi`. WSV and BfG records carry a PEGELONLINE number, used when an active PEGELONLINE station has it.
- **Declared provenance.** UHSLC and JODC records use tide-database's `sources/ticon/provenance.ts`, so a relay of a NOAA, Kartverket, JMA or JODC gauge goes to that originator, as tide-database already treats it. Other UHSLC records go to UHSLC itself.
- **Position.** RWS records, WSV and BfG records without a PEGELONLINE number, Marine Institute coastal (`mi_c`) records, and CMEMS records in countries whose feed has a list in the store (France's once REFMAR's is there) match an active station with levels: one within 50 m outright, else the best name match within 5 km (counting shared words, including ones like "buiten" that separate an outer harbor from an inner one), else the nearest within 1 km. Two equally good stations more than 100 m apart leave the record unmatched. Never by name alone, since RWS and CMEMS spell places differently. A CMEMS record linked to a station of its national feed (`LINKS` in `src/same-as.ts`) matches that station wherever CMEMS places it, or stays `to_match` if it isn't an active station with levels; the UK's match the EA first.
- **Same station.** A record still unmatched takes the match of another record in its tide-database station file, since tide-database merges records only when they're the same gauge.
- **UK gauges.** BODC's records, and UK CMEMS ones, match an EA gauge by position, as above, once the EA's list is in the store, and BODC's own gauge once its list is. NOC's records of gauges outside the UK network stay `restricted`: BODC's open data is the UK network's alone.
- **CMEMS elsewhere.** CMEMS records in countries whose feed has no list in the store yet are `to_match`: cleared once matched to one of that feed's stations, not before.
- **Irish river gauges.** Marine Institute river (`mi_r`) records are `unmatched`: their dataset names no datum, so the adapter doesn't list them.
- **Contributor.** The rest map by contributor to a planned source, with its license status from Open Waters' internal sources research. A record's own `commercial_use` is TICON's terms for its copy; the replacement comes from the originator, under the originator's terms.

"Ready" means a station matched. German records' PEGELONLINE history reaches back to 2000 at most, and not at all at gauges without a download, so the rest comes from GESLA's copies (`gesla`, whose backfill is running). "Planned" and "license" ids are built from codes and not yet checked against the source's station list.
